### Improved
- The compact sliders in the panel now respond wherever the track runs. Clicking on the value, the preset name, or an icon that has no button behind it sets the value at that spot — nothing swallows your click any more.
- Icons that *do* something keep doing it: the speaker still toggles sound, and the tap icon still mutes tap sounds. They are protected, everything else is track.
- Fixed a slider that could jump: a value you set by clicking is applied immediately, instead of being held back and landing later.

### Beta
- The window is now named, so screen-capture and streaming tools can find it in their window lists.
- Nothing is sent automatically — only what you paste and submit.
