### Improvements
- The panel and Settings have been redesigned — quieter surfaces, clearer hierarchy, and consistent focus behavior throughout.
- First run now guides you through pressure feel and pitch with a live preview, so the pen matches your hand before you start drawing.
- Hold-mute accepts more than one key: record any of them and the sound stays off only while a key is held.
- The Info tab shows screen-calibration diagnostics, and one click copies everything support needs (version, OS, Mac model, locale, license state).

### Sound
- Tap sounds now use per-tool tick banks, so dotting with a pencil, pen, marker, or crayon each has its own attack.
- Marker taps line up with the highlighter body instead of borrowing the pen's attack.

### Bug Fixes
- The app no longer quits when you draw right after switching audio device or sample rate. Drawing during the rebuild window is now safe, and sound returns on its own once the graph is back.
- Window and curve transitions no longer jitter when the panel resizes.
- The purchase link in Settings now opens the store correctly — it previously led to a missing page.

### Beta
- This is a beta build. Settings has a **Beta** tab with **Send Beta Feedback** and **Copy Diagnostics** — the form opens with your diagnostics already copied, so you only need to paste.
- Nothing is sent automatically — only what you paste and submit.
