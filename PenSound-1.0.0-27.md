### Sound

- **Crayon, pencil, and marker were re-made from the ground up.** Their source material was reworked, so the crayon's windy hiss is gone, the marker's squeak no longer wanders off pitch, and the pencil sits closer to the real thing.
- **A stroke now wears down once.** Crayon and pencil start each stroke fresh and wear as it goes, instead of cycling through the same texture — the way a real tip does.
- The crayon feels firmer and more solid, with more body behind it.
- Long pencil strokes lost the faint electronic edge they had picked up.
- Nothing changed for the pen — it sounds exactly as it did.

### Fixed

- A stroke that crosses over the palette keeps sounding. The sound follows the stroke, not the cursor: only strokes that *begin* on the palette stay quiet.
- On Windows, adjusting the palette with your pen is silent too — the same rule as on Mac.
- On Windows, the palette moves from its top bar only. Empty space in its body is no longer a drag handle, so a slightly missed slider leaves the window where it is. Right-click on the palette now does nothing; the tray icon still has the menu.

### Beta

- Nothing is sent automatically — only what you paste and submit.
