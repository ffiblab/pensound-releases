### Sound
- Fast strokes no longer surge in waves — paper-grain texture now stays even at any drawing speed.
- Sound response is now equally quick on every output device. Bluetooth headphones and 48kHz interfaces previously reacted noticeably slower than wired 96kHz output.
- Pen has a brand-new source recording: longer strokes no longer feel repetitive, and its tone stays steady instead of subtly wavering.
- Pencil texture is calmer at normal writing speed (less low-frequency pumping), while keeping its paper-grain character.
