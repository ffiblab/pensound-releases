### Improvements
- Brand-new menu bar icon — a pen nib with sound ripples that sits naturally alongside the system's template icons in both light and dark menu bars.
- The colored pencil tool has been retired to keep the palette focused on the most distinct pen textures.
- Beta testers get a fresh, full-length trial on first launch of 1.0.

### Sound Improvements
- Steady, constant-speed strokes now carry a natural grain-and-texture rhythm instead of sounding like uniform static — long straight lines feel far more like real friction on paper.
- Fixed quick taps and dabs that could randomly come out too quiet; short contacts now sound at a consistent, natural level.
