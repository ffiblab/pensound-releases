### Beta
- Sending a crash report is now a single paste. When the app notices that it quit unexpectedly, **Send Report…** copies a short crash summary together with your diagnostics and opens the feedback form — there is no folder to dig through any more.
- If no crash file was left behind (a forced quit or a power loss), the report simply opens with your diagnostics, exactly as before.
- The feedback form now opens in your language: Korean for a Korean interface, English everywhere else.
- Nothing is sent automatically — only what you paste and submit.
