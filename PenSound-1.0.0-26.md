### New
- **Space** — the sound moves with your pen. Try turning it on from **Space** in the palette; it comes through best with headphones.
- The palette can step aside now. **Show palette only in selected apps** keeps it on screen while you are in the apps you chose, and out of the way everywhere else.

### Sound
- Repeated pencil strokes are even again. Drawing the same short stroke over and over used to make one of them jump out; the paper grain is still there, only the per-stroke lottery is gone.
- The overall level was re-staged, and your saved volume comes across with it — nothing gets louder or quieter under you.
