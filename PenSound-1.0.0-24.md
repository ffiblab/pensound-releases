### Sound
- PenSound is much louder now. It used to stay quiet next to other apps even at 100% — that headroom is now yours.
- Your saved volume is lowered automatically to preserve the level you are used to — now you have room to turn it up.
- Very fast strokes and hard impacts are now held by the output ceiling, so an occasional stroke no longer jumps out.

### Improved
- The panel answers your first click. Reaching over from another app used to cost two clicks — one to wake the panel, one to act. Now the first click lands on the control you aimed at, including the start of a drag.
- While the panel is kept on top, it takes your hand but never your keyboard. The app you are drawing in keeps typing and shortcuts, so nothing changes underneath you. Keyboard control of the panel itself — Tab and the arrow keys — lives in the panel you open from the menu bar.
- Turning **Always on Top** on and off is now one quiet switch: no flicker, and the doubled shadow that used to appear for a moment while it changed is gone.

### Beta
- Nothing is sent automatically — only what you paste and submit.
